About me

I’m Bijam, a UI designer from Kathmandu. I focus on creating interfaces that look clean, feel smooth, and make sense the moment you see them.

I’m new to the industry, but I’ve been putting in the time. I care about the details — typography, spacing, color, and how everything comes together to shape the experience.

Most of my time goes into Figma, exploring ideas, studying design trends, and breaking down interfaces I like to understand what makes them work.
